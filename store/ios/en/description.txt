Welcome to the Fruit Village market! Drop fruits into the calabash, let two identical fruits touch and watch them merge into a bigger one. A fruit merge puzzle that is easy to pick up, relaxing to play and full of surprises.

🍊 A MERGE PUZZLE WITH AFRICAN FLAVOR
Two identical fruits that touch merge into a bigger fruit. From the tiny sidem to the monkey bread, discover 11 West African fruits: sidem, bissap, tamarind, ditakh, madd, guava, mango, papaya, pineapple, coconut and monkey bread (baobab fruit). Aim well, read the bounces and keep some room: if the calabash overflows, the game is over.

🌳 THE BAOBAB MOMENT
Merge two monkey breads to trigger the Baobab Moment: slow motion, a giant baobab grows behind the market, coins rain down and sabar drums roll! The best moment in the game. Can you earn it?

💥 CASCADING COMBOS
One merge triggers another… and another. Chain merges to multiply your points: COMBO x2, x3, x4! Each merge plays its own note, higher for bigger fruits, so your combos turn into a melody.

📖 THE FRUIT ALBUM
Every new fruit you reach unlocks its "Did you know?" card. Did you know bissap is used to make Senegal's national drink? That ditakh is packed with vitamin C? That monkey bread is the fruit of the baobab, the tree where elders gather to talk? Complete the album and learn while you play.

🎯 DAILY MISSIONS AND DAILY GIFT
New missions every day (combos, merges, fruits to reach) plus a daily login gift. Keep your streak going to earn more coins.

🪄 HANDY POWER-UPS
Stuck? Shake the calabash, crush an annoying fruit with the pestle, swap the next fruit or make it grow one size. Power-ups are bought with coins earned by playing.

🌅 A LIVELY 3D MARKET
3D fruits with faces that smile, wince or panic as the calabash fills up, a mischievous monkey mascot, a bustling market, and day, sunset and night moods.
