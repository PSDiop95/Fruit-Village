The app has no account registration or login, no user-generated content, and no paid content or In-App Purchases.

**2. Purpose and target audience**
Fruit Village is a casual "merge" puzzle game for a general audience (rated 4+), for players who enjoy short, relaxing sessions of 3 to 10 minutes. The player drops fruits into a calabash; two identical fruits that touch merge into a bigger one, and the game ends when the calabash overflows. Its value is light entertainment combined with cultural discovery: the 11 fruits come from West Africa (bissap, ditakh, madd, baobab fruit…) and each new fruit unlocks a short "Did you know?" card about it.

**3. Setup and access to main features**
No setup, account or credentials are needed:
- Play: tap "Play" on the title screen. Drag a finger to aim, release to drop the fruit.
- Fruit Album, Missions, Daily gift and Settings: buttons on the title screen.
- Power-ups (shake, pestle, swap, grow): buttons during a game, paid with coins earned by playing.
- Optional rewarded ads: "Continue" on the game-over screen and "Double coins" on the summary screen. Ads are never required to play.
Some features unlock progressively as the player levels up (XP earned at the end of each game): daily missions at level 2, power-ups at level 3, daily gift at level 4 and the Fruit Album at level 5. The first game already gives level 2; level 5 takes roughly 10 to 15 games. The attached recording shows all of these features.

**4. External services**
- Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to show ads, mostly optional rewarded ads.
- Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to collect ad consent where required (EEA, UK, Switzerland).
- Apple App Tracking Transparency to ask permission before personalized ads.
The game itself runs fully offline. There are no data providers, login, payment processors or AI services.

**5. Regional differences**
The app works the same way in all regions. The only difference is the ad consent form, which Google UMP shows only where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and the UK). The app is available in English and French.

Not applicable. The app is not in a regulated industry. All artwork, 3D models, music and texts were created for the app or come from royalty-free sources.
